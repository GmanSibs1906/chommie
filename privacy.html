Chommie

Terms and privacy

Children's information

Learners' names, marks and exercise books are children's personal information under POPIA. Chommie processes a learner's book only when a parent or guardian has given specific, informed consent. Books without consent are not photographed, and consent can be withdrawn per learner at any time.

Names stay in the classroom

Names are covered on the teacher's phone before a photo is uploaded. Learners are record numbers. The link between number and name stays on the teacher's phone and is used only to print homework.

Photos are deleted

Photos are deleted from the phone once the teacher confirms the marks. At the end of the pilot everything is purged and the school receives a deletion report.

AI vendors outside South Africa

Photos are read through OpenRouter by models from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI. This routing is global and not in South Africa. The demo uses made-up learners only. A real pilot needs a decision under POPIA section 72 on transfers abroad.

The demo

The demo on Saturday 14 November 2026 uses made-up learners and made-up books. No real learner data is collected.
