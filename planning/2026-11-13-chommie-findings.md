# Findings: Chommie

_Written 2026-11-13 by Frank from 18 sources._

## In short

Chommie is an installable phone web app for South African teachers who work to CAPS and the ATP. It runs one weekly loop: plan the term from the ATP, teach, print homework for each learner, photograph the class's books, mark them with two AI vendors and a Reviewer, show a learner-by-topic heat map, and re-plan tomorrow's lesson. The owner is a teacher with a large class and heavy admin. The HOD sees an overview. Learners have no devices and only ever get printed homework with their name on it. It is a competition entry by Team Albert AI (Colleague Zero), judged by Francois van der Merwe of Otinga.io, whose public headline is about ROI from AI. The demo is on Saturday 14 November 2026 and uses made-up Grade 6 Maths, Term 4 fractions data. First finding: marking photos of handwriting is not unique. Teachr, Marking.ai, CoGrader, EduFlare, MarkEase and The Marking App all claim it, and teacher approval before release is standard. So the pitch has to lead with what nobody else shows: ATP-aware planning and re-planning, printed homework for each learner, the HOD view, SA-SAMS-ready marks, and flagging unreadable answers instead of guessing. Second: learners' named books are children's personal information under POPIA. A pilot needs parent consent, name masking before any AI call, quick deletion and a clear answer on sending data abroad, because Claude has no in-Region option in Cape Town. Third: build it as an offline-tolerant web app that works on Android first, because most teachers use Android, iPhones handle the camera differently, and data and printing cost teachers money. Fourth: the competition rules are not public, so build only the scripted demo path and get the rules from Otinga directly.

## Build

- Upload plan screen: photograph the ATP and the school teaching planner, or pick the official ATP. - Compressed-term re-planner that merges or shortens ATP topics to reach the next formal task date. — Teachers describe a 10-week programme expected in six weeks. _(Research: South African teachers marking workload — research how South African teachers working to CAPS and the ATP actually mark, plan and report, and what they say about it. ## Proposed work

- [ ] Add an 'unreadable' state for each answer: crop the unclear handwriting, show it to the teacher in a review queue, and give no mark until the teacher decides. — Marking.ai's selling points include 'What happens when it is unsure?', and SA reviewers complain about marking accuracy. Neither SA competitor page says it flags illegible work.
- [ ] Show the cropped photo of the learner's working next to every mark, so the teacher sees what earned or lost it. — Marking.ai quotes 'the words that earned each mark' as its explainability feature. Chommie needs the same proof for handwritten books.
- [ ] Add a teacher approval step: no marks, homework or HOD numbers go out until the teacher signs off on the class batch. — Marking.ai ('nothing reaches a student until a teacher approves it') and Fixter both make teacher control the core promise, and SA complaints centre on unexplained AI judgements.
- [ ] Export marks as an SA-SAMS-ready CSV for each class and assessment, for the school clerk to import. — SA-SAMS runs as a local school database, and other platforms 'wait on manual CSVs that arrive late or incomplete'.
- [ ] Let teachers photograph or upload a bought test and memo (e.g. from Teacha!) and turn it into a mark scheme Chommie can mark against. — SA teachers already buy CAPS term papers with memoranda, e.g. 'Vraestel 1 en Memorandum R38.00'.
- [ ] Export marks as an SA-SAMS-compatible Excel mark-sheet: keep the exported file name with the class letter added before .xls, enter -1 for a valid absence, refuse to save with blank cells, and lock task totals and dates before export. — The EMIS guideline sets these exact rules, says a sheet can be imported only once, and says task totals and dates cannot be changed after import.
- [ ] Add an HOD sign-off step: a printable record sheet for HOD signature, with the export to the SA-SAMS admin locked until the HOD approves. — 'Marks still need to be controlled by HOD'; 'Educator may print from excel mark-sheet for HOD signing'; 'Administrators may import marks after HOD approval'.
- [ ] Tag every task as formal or informal. Only formal tasks go to the record sheet and export; informal classwork feeds the 'who is struggling' view only. — CAPS says informal daily tasks are not formally recorded, while formal tasks are recorded and moderated.
- [ ] Build a moderation evidence pack per formal task (memo, mark list and photos of the marked workbook pages) that the SMT or HOD can open. — Formal task evidence 'must be visible in learners' workbooks' and all formal tasks are subject to internal moderation by the SMT.
- [ ] Add a compressed-term re-planner: when weeks are lost, Term Planner shows which ATP topics to merge or shorten to still reach the next formal task date. — A former teacher left after being expected to finish a 10-week programme in six weeks.
- [ ] Show a print-cost preview before printing per-learner homework (page count and a compact layout option). — Teachers 'are often forced to buy materials out of their own pockets', and some schools got no Section 21 allocation.
- [ ] On-device masking: crop or blur the name strip on each book photo and replace it with a class-local code before any image leaves the phone. The code-to-name map stays on the teacher's device. — Names with education records are personal information (DBE policy quoting POPIA). The court barred publishing learner names with results. Vendors already mask PII before it reaches the model. (ENS opinion: a pseudonymous code is still personal information, so this lowers risk but does not remove POP
- [ ] Automatic deletion of book photos after marking is confirmed, plus an end-of-pilot purge with a deletion report the school can keep. — The DBE policy commits to keeping personal information only as long as legally required. Section 19 safeguards also mean less data held means less at risk.
- [ ] Per-learner report delivery that checks the recipient before sending; never bulk-attach other learners' reports. — A parent reports a school sending 21 other learners' academic reports to them.
- [ ] A section 19 risk register and safeguards sheet for the pilot (content). It lists foreseeable risks such as a lost phone, a vendor breach or a misdirected report, the safeguard for each, and the review date. — The Guidance Note on section 19(1) requires identifying foreseeable risks, maintaining safeguards and updating them. The Dis-Chem breach came through a third-party-run service.
- [ ] Build a legibility gate: each transcribed answer gets a confidence score, and anything below the threshold is marked 'unreadable – teacher to check' instead of being marked. — Users already enforce an 80% confidence rule in prompts. Developers name hard-to-read handwriting and crossed-out work as the main blocker.
- [ ] Mask the learner-name area on each photo and replace it with a learner number before any image is sent to a marking vendor. Restore the names locally for printed homework and reports. — A teacher marking with Claude keeps names from ever reaching the AI. Microsoft's doc warns that personal data needs careful attention.
