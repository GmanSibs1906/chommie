# Findings: Chommie

_Written 2026-11-14 by Frank from 18 sources._

## In short

Chommie is a phone-first web app for South African teachers who teach to CAPS and the ATP. It is being built as Team Albert AI's competition entry, judged by Francois van der Merwe of Otinga.io, and the hand-in is 11:00 on 14 November 2026. It runs one weekly loop. It reads the ATP, puts the term on real school days, writes lessons and printed homework for each learner, photographs and marks exercise books with two AI markers and a Reviewer, shows a learner-by-topic heat map, and uses the 40% rule to re-plan tomorrow's lesson. The owner is a teacher with a large class. The HOD sees an overview. Learners have no devices and only ever get paper. The demo is Grade 6 Maths, Term 4 fractions, with made-up learners and a 'Load demo run' button as the backup. First, reading handwriting from photos is now common: Teachr, Marking.ai, CoGrader, EduFlare, MarkEase and The Marking App all do it, and teacher approval is standard. So Chommie has to lead with ATP re-planning, flagging unreadable answers instead of guessing, printed homework for each learner, and the HOD sign-off and SA-SAMS hand-off. Second, EduFlare is the closest local rival. It has rand prices with VAT included, hosting in South Africa, a POPIA operator annexure, a report for each learner and a 'Focus next' list, so Chommie's per-learner claims must be sharper than 'personal feedback'. Third, named exercise books are children's personal information under POPIA, and Claude cannot be kept in Cape Town. Consent, name masking, deletion and the section 72 transfer question will decide whether the pilot can run. Fourth, the look should follow thwipe.ai: a calm off-white page with a serif headline and the action first. It should avoid the heavy gradients and mixed fonts the crawler flagged on most competitors.

## Proposed work

- [ ] Add an 'unreadable' state for each answer: crop the unclear handwriting, show it to the teacher in a review queue, and give no mark until the teacher decides. — Marking.ai's selling points include 'What happens when it is unsure?', and SA reviewers complain about marking accuracy. Neither SA competitor page says it flags illegible work.
- [ ] Show the cropped photo of the learner's working next to every mark, so the teacher sees what earned or lost it. — Marking.ai quotes 'the words that earned each mark' as its explainability feature. Chommie needs the same proof for handwritten books.
- [ ] Add a teacher approval step: no marks, homework or HOD numbers go out until the teacher signs off on the class batch. — Marking.ai ('nothing reaches a student until a teacher approves it') and Fixter both make teacher control the core promise, and SA complaints centre on unexplained AI judgements.
- [ ] Export marks as an SA-SAMS-ready CSV for each class and assessment, for the school clerk to import. — SA-SAMS runs as a local school database, and other platforms 'wait on manual CSVs that arrive late or incomplete'.
- [ ] Let teachers photograph or upload a bought test and memo (e.g. from Teacha!) and turn it into a mark scheme Chommie can mark against. — SA teachers already buy CAPS term papers with memoranda, e.g. 'Vraestel 1 en Memorandum R38.00'.
- [ ] Export marks as an SA-SAMS-compatible Excel mark-sheet: keep the exported file name with the class letter added before .xls, enter -1 for a valid absence, refuse to save with blank cells, and lock task totals and dates before export. — The EMIS guideline sets these exact rules, says a sheet can be imported only once, and says task totals and dates cannot be changed after import.
- [ ] Add an HOD sign-off step: a printable record sheet for HOD signature, with the export to the SA-SAMS admin locked until the HOD approves. — 'Marks still need to be controlled by HOD'; 'Educator may print from excel mark-sheet for HOD signing'; 'Administrators may import marks after HOD approval'.
- [ ] Tag every task as formal or informal. Only formal tasks go to the record sheet and export; informal classwork feeds the 'who is struggling' view only. — CAPS says informal daily tasks are not formally recorded, while formal tasks are recorded and moderated.
- [ ] Build a moderation evidence pack per formal task (memo, mark list and photos of the marked workbook pages) that the SMT or HOD can open. — Formal task evidence 'must be visible in learners' workbooks' and all formal tasks are subject to internal moderation by the SMT.
- [ ] Add a compressed-term re-planner: when weeks are lost, Term Planner shows which ATP topics to merge or shorten to still reach the next formal task date. — A former teacher left after being expected to finish a 10-week programme in six weeks.
- [ ] Show a print-cost preview before printing per-learner homework (page count and a compact layout option). — Teachers 'are often forced to buy materials out of their own pockets', and some schools got no Section 21 allocation.
- [ ] On-device masking: crop or blur the name strip on each book photo and replace it with a class-local code before any image leaves the phone.
