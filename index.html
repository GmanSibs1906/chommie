Plain answers.

Is it run by AI or by people?

Both, and the teacher has the last word. AI drafts the plan, lessons and marks. Plain code does every total, percentage and date. You approve before anything is used.

What if it cannot read a learner's handwriting?

It says so. An answer it cannot read with confidence gets no mark and goes to you with a cropped picture of the answer.

Why two markers?

Two AI markers from different companies mark every book. Where they disagree, a Reviewer re-checks, and you make the final call.

What happens to learners' names and photos?

Names are covered on your phone before a photo is sent, and learners are record numbers. Photos are deleted once you confirm the marks. Only learners whose parent or guardian has signed consent are photographed, and consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Where does the data go?

Photos are read by AI services outside South Africa. That is acceptable for the demo, which uses made-up learners only. The pilot needs a decision under POPIA section 72 first.

Do learners need a phone?

No. Learners only receive printed homework with their name on it.

Does it work to CAPS and the ATP?

Yes. Every lesson is tagged to its ATP week and nothing outside your ATP or CAPS is added. The demo covers Grade 6 Maths, Term 4, fractions.

Does it connect to SA-SAMS?

Through a file. Chommie makes an Excel mark sheet for the HOD to sign, and only then lets you export it for SA-SAMS.
