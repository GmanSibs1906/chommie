# Chommie: the teacher's buddy (Team Albert AI, Colleague Zero)

Chosen by Gman, 13 Nov 2026. Presentation: Desktop/Chommie-presentation/Chommie.html.

## Pitch
"Every night, teachers carry home piles of books to mark and plan. Chommie ends that. Snap the class's books with your phone. Chommie marks them, checks its own marks, shows exactly who is struggling, and rewrites tomorrow's lesson to fix it. The teacher teaches. Chommie does the rest."

## The problem (sourced)
- 70% of SA teachers name admin as their top stressor (Stellenbosch University survey, 2025).
- Some Gauteng Grade 1–2 classes have 87–92 learners (news reports, Feb 2025).
- 81% of Grade 4 learners cannot read for meaning (PIRLS 2021).

## The journey
1. Upload the plan: photograph the ATP + the school's teaching planner, or pick the official one. Chommie reads weeks, topics, test dates.
2. Plan the whole term from the ATP + the school's teaching plan: every topic on real school days, every test and deadline date known; each lesson gets an outline, examples, class work, homework. The plan changes when topics go badly, but Chommie always works back from the deadlines so everything required is still covered in time.
3. Teacher teaches. Print, edit in the app, or change by voice note.
4. Personalised homework: each learner gets homework on exactly what they got wrong (stretch questions for those ahead). Chommie wrote it, so it knows every answer. Printed per learner with their name (no learner devices); the teacher can see and change any learner's homework first.
5. Snap the books: Chommie marks every answer; a second agent re-checks.
6. Report: class heat map, each learner × topic in green/amber/red.
7. Re-plan only when needed — the 40% rule: per topic, a learner below 50% is struggling. Under 40% of the class struggling → personal homework fixes it and the lesson plan stays. 40% or more → Chommie adds a short class re-teach to an upcoming lesson, never drops a required ATP topic, finishes before the assessment date, warns early if time is short. The teacher can change both numbers.
8. Repeat weekly until the term goal is met.

## Agents
1. Coordinator: takes the goal, decides each next step, hands out jobs, re-plans.
2. Curriculum Reader: ATP photos → weeks/topics/test dates, checked against official CAPS.
3. Term Planner: puts topics on real school days, keeps pace.
4. Lesson Writer: lessons, class work, each learner's personal homework, answer memos.
5. Marker: reads photos of learners' books, marks against the memo.
6. Reviewer: independent check of everything; sends work back to be fixed.
7. Analyst: heat map; applies the 40% rule (personal homework vs class re-teach).
The teacher approves (Chommie drafts, the teacher decides).

## No made-up answers
Only topics from uploaded ATP/CAPS (each lesson tagged to its ATP week). Code does totals/percentages/dates. Memo answers proven by calculation. Two markers from different AI vendors; disagreement goes to the teacher. Unclear handwriting is flagged, never guessed. Nothing skips the Reviewer (two failed fixes, then the teacher). Teacher approval. Every step logged.

## Judges (Francois van der Merwe, Otinga.io)
One workflow (the teaching loop), one owner (the teacher; HOD sees the overview), one number (learners moving red→green with the ATP finished on time). 30-day pilot: one school, one grade, one subject.

## Demo slice
Grade 6 Maths, Term 4, fractions: photos of real-looking books → marks → Reviewer catches a wrong mark live → heat map → tomorrow's lesson re-plans → voice note "make it shorter".
