WHAT GMAN WANTS (his words, summarised):
1. Simple login: any email and any password work (demo only; store locally). No real auth.
2. Teacher dashboard = their classes. A teacher may teach several classes, grades and subjects. "Create class": name (e.g. 6B), grade, subject. Each class has its OWN dashboard, ATP, term plan, learners, marking, homework and heat map.
3. ATP, never hard-coded: the teacher picks GRADE then SUBJECT (and term) from the real DBE library and Chommie loads that ATP. Use data/dbe-atp-catalog.json (514 official DBE ATP documents, Grades 1-12, all subjects, with `source_url` on education.gov.za). Fetch the chosen PDF through a Netlify Function (server-side, avoids CORS; cache it, e.g. Netlify Blobs), extract the text (pdf.js or similar), and have the Curriculum Reader (Claude Sonnet 5.5) turn it into weeks, topics, hours and assessment dates for the chosen term. ALSO: "Upload ATP (PDF or photos)" for a teacher's own file. Remove the "Up to 3 photos" wording; a real ATP is a multi-page PDF. Test with tests/fixtures/grade-6-maths-atp-caps.pdf (Gman's real Grade 6 Maths ATP, 5 pages, all 4 terms): uploading it must produce the real Term 4 topics from the document, not built-in data. Built-in Grade 6 data may remain ONLY as the fallback behind "Load demo run".
4. Learners: add by typing (first name, surname) OR by taking a photo of a class list, which the AI reads into names for the teacher to confirm and edit. Keep the consent switch per learner.
5. Mobile-first and simple for teachers who are not tech-savvy: big buttons, few words, one clear next step. Marking flow: open the class -> Mark -> pick the learner -> take the photo -> pick the next learner -> photo ... Show who is done and who is still to do.
6. Homework marking: open a learner -> see their homework rounds with Marked / Not marked -> pick the round -> take the photo. Also a class view of homework status.
7. Class overview and heat map per class.
8. AGENT LABELS for the demo: on every screen, a neat card in the site's style saying which agent is working there and what it does, e.g. "Term Planner agent: puts every ATP topic on a real school day and works back from test and exam dates." Agents: Coordinator, Curriculum Reader, Term Planner, Lesson Writer, Marker A + Marker B (two companies), Reviewer, Analyst. When an agent is working, show it (e.g. "Marker B (Google) is marking 6B-014...").
9. Keep "Load demo run" (a ready-made demo class, labelled as made-up) as the stage backup.
