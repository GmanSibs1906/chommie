# Chommie: the teacher's buddy

Team Albert AI · Colleague Zero (Melsoft Academy × GDG Pretoria), 13 to 14 November 2026.

## What it does

South African teachers lose their evenings marking books and planning lessons by hand. Chommie is a digital colleague that takes that work off them.

**Plans the whole term.** The teacher photographs the ATP (Annual Teaching Plan) and the school's teaching planner. Chommie puts every topic on a real school day, with every test and deadline in place.

**Marks the books.** After a lesson, the teacher snaps the learners' books with a phone. Chommie marks every answer against the memo. A second agent, the Reviewer, checks every mark. Disagreements and unclear handwriting go to the teacher; nothing is guessed.

**Shows who is struggling.** A heat map shows every learner against every topic in green, amber and red.

**Re-plans.** When a topic goes badly, Chommie adjusts the plan, never dropping a required ATP topic and always finishing before the assessment date.

The teacher approves everything. Demo: Grade 6 Maths, Term 4, fractions, with made-up learners only.

## The agents

Coordinator, Curriculum Reader, Term Planner, Lesson Writer, Marker, Reviewer and Analyst. Each has one job; the Reviewer checks the others' work before the teacher sees it.
